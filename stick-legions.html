New in Stick Legions II

A big update with the same art style. Valley of Gold and The Republic of the Order are both still here, and if you saved in V1.6 on this website, your chapters stay unlocked.

Bigger map

Twice as long

Drag the battlefield left and right with your finger or mouse (or use the arrow keys) to see your base, your legions and the enemy. The minimap at the top shows everyone. Tap it to jump there.

▲ ▼ buttons

Your army at a glance

The top bar trains units. Tap ▼ to see a card for each unit with how many are on the field. Tap ▲ to go back. A button goes see-through when you can't go further.

Abilities

Every unit has one

Tap an army card to open that unit's ability bar, then tap the ability. ↩ takes you back. Swordwrath RAGE, Archidon FIRE ARROWS, Spearton SHIELD WALL, Giant GROUND SMASH, Horsemen CHARGE, Meric BIG HEAL.

Miner ability

Spike walls

Miners can build a small wooden spike wall in front of your army for 150 gold, up to 2 at a time. Enemies have to break it before they reach you. The Ambush wall is smaller now too.

Shop

Rupis and Gold skins

Every battle gives Rupis: 20 for a win, 5 for a loss. Spend 1000 Rupis on a Gold skin for the Miner, Swordwrath, Archidon, Spearton, Giant or Horsemen. Skins only change the look. Tap a skin you own to turn it on or off.

Online

Scan to join

When you HOST on this website, a QR code appears. Your friend scans it with a phone or tablet camera and joins your room automatically, no typing.

New in V1.5: The Republic of the Order

A second campaign set in Rome, the city the Elder built. You can play it right away, without finishing Valley of Gold.

Chapter 1

The Elder founds Rome. Train Horsemen and defend the new city.

Chapter 2 · Ambush

Chapter 3

Meet the Meric, a medic with a medkit who heals your soldiers from behind the front line.

Chapter 4 · Reverse Ambush

Storm the Palisade

Chapter 5

One Peninsula

The final battle to unite Italy. Then the story turns to Sicily, Carthage and the next campaign: The Order Empire.

Campaign menu

Pick your campaign

Tap CAMPAIGN, then choose Valley of Gold (Venetia) or The Republic of the Order (Rome).

New in V1.6: Save your game

After you win a chapter, tap SAVE to keep your progress, or CONTINUE to play on. Your save stays on the device and browser you played on: a save on one iPad does not appear on another.

Quick Battle modes

Tap QUICK BATTLE and choose how you fight.

Normal

The classic battle: destroy the enemy statue.

Ambush

The enemy has no statue or gold mine, only a spike wall. Survive their waves until sunrise, or break the wall.

Reverse Ambush

You start with a big army and a chest of gold, but no miners. Break their spike wall before time runs out.

How to play

The easiest way: play it right here in your browser, nothing to download (or play on Scratch). Or download the file and open it yourself:

  1. Download the game with the button above.
  2. Go to scratch.mit.edu, tap Create, then File → Load from your computer and pick the file.
  3. Press the green flag. Pick Campaign and start Chapter 1.

Multiplayer

Tap MULTIPLAYER on the main menu and play online against a friend. 2 players on one screen is no longer in Stick Legions II: use the Play V1.6 button at the bottom of the page for that.

New in V1.6

Modes and new units

Tap MODE on the multiplayer screen to pick Normal, Ambush (Red ambushes Blue from behind a spike wall) or Reverse Ambush (Blue attacks Red's wall with a big army). Both sides can train Horsemen and Merics. Online, the host's mode is used.

Online

Play a friend

One player taps HOST and gets a room code (and a QR code on this website), the other scans it, taps JOIN and types it, or uses FIND SERVER to see open games and tap one. Works right here on stick-manx.github.io with no account, or on scratch.mit.edu when both players are Scratchers.
