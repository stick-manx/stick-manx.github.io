How to play

The easiest way: play on Scratch right in your browser, nothing to download. Or download the file and open it yourself:

  1. Download the game with the button above.
  2. Go to scratch.mit.edu, tap Create, then File → Load from your computer and pick the file.
  3. Press the green flag. Pick Campaign and start Chapter 1.

Multiplayer

New in V1.2. Tap MULTIPLAYER on the main menu.

Same screen

2 Players

Blue vs Red on one laptop or tablet. Blue uses the left buttons (keys 1-5, A, D), Red uses the right buttons (keys 6-0, K, L).

Online

Play a friend

One player taps HOST and gets a room code, the other taps JOIN and types it, or uses FIND SERVER to see open games and tap one. Works on scratch.mit.edu when both players are logged in as Scratchers.

Controls

Switch between them in Settings, along with music and the narrator voice.

Touch screen

Bottom
Tap a unit button to train it
Top
Tap DEFEND or ATTACK (your army shouts it!)
Red ✕
Cancel a unit you queued, gold back
MENU
Pause: Resume, Settings or Resign
Story
Tap the screen for the next line, or SKIP

PC / keys

1 – 5
Train Miner, Sword, Archer, Spear, Giant
A / D
Attack / Defend
X
Cancel the last queued unit
P
Pause / resume
Space
Next story line
